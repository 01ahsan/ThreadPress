# ThreadPress privacy policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

ThreadPress collects no data. Everything it does happens inside your browser, on your device.

## What ThreadPress reads

When you click ThreadPress or its export button, it reads the conversation shown on the page so it can turn it into a file. It reads only the messages of the open chat. It does not read the sidebar, your other chats, your account name or email, cookies, passwords, or browser history.

## What ThreadPress stores or sends

Nothing. ThreadPress:

- makes no network requests. There are no analytics, tracking, error reports, ads or update checks.
- does not save your conversations or settings anywhere.
- does not use the chat sites' private APIs or your login session.

The files you export are saved only where your browser saves downloads. Image links are left out of exports unless you turn them on, because such links can contain private access codes.

## Permissions

The browser extension asks for no permissions. It runs only on the supported chat sites (chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, grok.com, x.com/i/grok, chat.deepseek.com, kimi.com, kimi.moonshot.cn) so it can read the conversation you are exporting.

## Bug reports

The "Save page layout" option creates a file describing the page's structure, with all chat text removed. It is saved to your device and shared only if you choose to attach it to a bug report.
