ThreadPress

Export AI chat conversations to Markdown, JSON or plain text. Free, open source, and it never sends anything anywhere.

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

ThreadPress

  1. Open a conversation on a supported site.
  2. Click the ThreadPress bookmark. A small panel appears.
  3. Pick a format and click Export whole chat. To save only part of a chat, highlight it first.

Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (⌘+Shift+B on Mac). On a phone, copy the code below into a new bookmark's address, then type the bookmark's name in the address bar to run it.

Privacy

ThreadPress runs only when you click it, only on the page you are viewing, and only on your device. It makes no network requests, reads no cookies or logins, stores nothing, and leaves your name and account details out of exports.
