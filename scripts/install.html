Chrome, Edge or Brave

Takes about a minute, once.

Download ThreadPress
  1. Open the downloaded file and choose Extract all. On a Mac, just double-click it. You get a folder called ThreadPress.
  2. In the address bar, type chrome://extensions and press Enter. On Edge, type edge://extensions. On Brave, brave://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode. It's a switch at the top right (on Edge, at the left).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the ThreadPress folder.

Firefox

Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar.

ThreadPress

Can't see the bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl + Shift + B (Mac: ⌘ + Shift + B).

How to use it

Open any chat, then:

  • 1. Click Export chatThe button on the right edge of the chat (your bookmark in Firefox).
  • 2. Pick a formatPDF, Word, Markdown, HTML, LaTeX, text or JSON.
  • 3. Click ExportWhole chat, or just the messages you tick.

Want just part of a chat? Highlight it first.

Your chats stay yours. ThreadPress works entirely in your browser. It sends nothing anywhere, needs no account, and never includes your name, email or other chats in the file.
